## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the atmospheric depth of modern dark anime aesthetics with the prestige and gamified visual architecture of Steam community profiles. It targets gamers, digital collectors, and anime enthusiasts who prize visual drama, status expression, and cataloging personal identity.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Skeuomorphism / Modern Dark Slate** with **Graphic Manga Precision**:
- **Atmospheric Foundation:** Inky, deep slate backdrops evocative of late-night terminal screens and anime nightscapes.
- **Graphic Precision:** High-contrast linework, razor-sharp edge definitions, and screentone-inspired framing that honor manga layouts.
- **Gamified Prestige:** Showcased achievements, status tiering, level emblems, and collectible badges reminiscent of classic platform profiles, re-engineered through a contemporary, streamlined lens.
- **Emotional Cadence:** Immersive, focused, high-stakes, and rewarding. The UI should make every user feel like an elite operator curating their personal lore.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict darkness, utilizing stepped slate values to establish spatial planes without sacrificing contrast.

- **Primary (`#e63946` - Crimson Spark):** Used for critical primary actions, critical state indicators, health/vital metrics, and focal achievement unlocks. Never dilute with opacity; preserve its vibrant, razor-sharp edge.
- **Secondary (`#8b5cf6` - Violet Relic):** Reserved for prestige components, high-tier profile badges, level progression rings, and rare collectible highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#38bdf8` - Cyan Echo):** Accent channel for hyper-active terminal reads, system telemetry, online presence, and active link states.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy:**
  - Base Canvas (`#0e1117`): Deep midnight slate. Absorbs ambient light and grounds the screen.
  - Surface Mid (`#161b26`): Card containers, panel plates, and recessed trays.
  - Surface Elevated (`#1f2636`): Interactive cards, popovers, hovering layers, and control surfaces.
  - Manga Inset / Borders (`#2e384d` to `#ffffff` at variable opacities): Provides decisive separation lines that emulate inked comic panels.
- **Text & Foreground:** Crisp pure whites (`#f8fafc`) for primary headers and high-contrast readings, graded down to cold silver (`#94a3b8`) for metadata and secondary labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends stark technical geometry with razor-sharp editorial legibility.

- **Headline Font (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a sharp, slightly mechanical posture tailored for user handles, achievement titles, showcase modules, and rank headers.
- **Body Font (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Delivers uncompromised clarity for profile bios, comment feeds, game patch notes, and item inspect logs.
- **Label & Data Font (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical data points, level counters, timestamp readouts, XP percentages, and collectible rarity stamps.

All uppercase labels must utilize expanded letter-spacing (`0.05em` to `0.1em`) to mimic mecha HUD telemetry and manga chapter markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a high-density, 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around display showcases, persistent profile headers, and collectible badge matrices.

### Breakpoints & Layout Model
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Asymmetric profile matrix. Left/Primary column (8 cols) hosts the hero showcase, art banners, and active game activity; Right/Auxiliary column (4 cols) anchors the persistent steam-style profile summary (avatar, level circle, badge showcase, friend list, trade links).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Reflows into a single fluid 12-column layout. The profile summary moves directly under the banner showcase, stacking neatly before achievement grids.
- **Mobile (<767px):** Full-width, stacked single-column view with tight margins (`margin: 1rem`). Showcase carousels collapse into horizontally scrollable rails with snap points.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing operates on a strict base-4 rhythm. Elements are packed purposefully to mirror classic dense hub layouts, but separated by precise structural dividers (`1px` ink lines) rather than large voids of empty space.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, muddy atmospheric drop shadows in favor of crisp manga paneling, directional inner glows, and layered slate planar depth.

- **Manga Panel Borders:** Primary depth is produced through razor-thin borders (`1px` solid `#222938` for idle panels, transitioning to `#e63946` or `#8b5cf6` for focused/highlighted containers).
- **Surface Tiering:**
  - Base: Canvas `#0e1117`
  - Recessed/Trough (e.g., Progress bars, item slots): `#080a0e` with a 1px inner top border.
  - Floating Plate (Cards, Profile Modules): `#161b26`
  - High Plate (Modals, Dropdowns): `#1f2636` with a hard `0 12px 0 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` directional drop drop-plane.
- **Backlight Radiance:** Rare and legendary badge assets employ a localized, tight neon halo (`0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.45)`) to signal prestige without blooming the entire UI screen.

## Shapes

The design language favors decisive, sharp edges with a micro-radius (`roundedness: 1` = `0.25rem` / `4px`). This slight softening keeps the UI modern and usable without eroding the aggressive, inked silhouette of manga paneling and mechanical gaming HUDs.

- **Cards & Showcase Frames:** Micro-rounded corners (`4px`) with full-perimeter 1px borders.
- **Badges & Hex Tiles:** Polygon-clipped or angled bevels (45-degree chamfers on select prestige modules) to reinforce cyber-anime hardware detailing.
- **Level Counters:** Contained in circular or beveled diamond frames to establish distinct focal landmarks across user profiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Crimson Trigger):** Solid `#e63946` fill, `#ffffff` bold text in `JetBrains Mono`, 4px radius. On hover: shifts to `#f04754` with a crisp 1px pure white inner stroke. Active: shifts downward by 1px.
- **Secondary (Slate Inset):** Background `#1f2636`, 1px border in `#2e384d`, text `#f8fafc`. On hover: border shifts to `#e63946`, background lightens to `#262f42`.
- **Prestige Action (Violet Transcend):** Background `#8b5cf6`, crisp white text, ambient secondary halo glow. Reserved for trade confirmations, badge craft completions, and profile upgrades.

### Achievement Cards (Steam-Style Otaku Edition)
- Dark plate container (`#161b26`) framed with a 1px border (`#222938`).
- Left flank: 64x64px high-contrast icon frame with unlock status. Unlocked items feature vivid crimson/violet art; locked items remain 40% desaturated slate with a lock glyph.
- Center: Title in `Space Grotesk` (semi-bold, `#f8fafc`), description in `Hanken Grotesk` (`#94a3b8`), unlock timestamp in `JetBrains Mono` (`#e63946`).
- Right flank: Global rarity percentage tag styled as a monospace pill (`#080a0e` background, `#38bdf8` text).

### Level Counters & Badges
- **Level Ring:** Double-ring concentric circle. The outer ring displays the circular progress bar filled with crimson or violet; inner plate houses the numeric level in `Space Grotesk` bold (`#ffffff`).
- **Profile Badges:** Geometric shields (32x32px or 48x48px) with metallic foil borders, dynamic level tier ribbons, and rarity glow shaders.

### Chips & Rarity Tags
- Height: 24px, radius: 2px (clipped look), font: `label-badge` (10px uppercase `JetBrains Mono`).
- Common: Neutral slate (`#1f2636`), border `#2e384d`.
- Rare: Cyan stroke (`#38bdf8`), dark background `#0c1b29`.
- Mythic / Anime Exclusive: Crimson stroke (`#e63946`), glow pulse, text `#f8fafc`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Flat `#0e1117` background with a crisp 1px `#222938` boundary. Text is high-contrast white. Active focus swaps border immediately to `#e63946` with zero soft box-shadow blur.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Angular 14x14px square boxes with 2px borders. Selected state fills with solid `#e63946` containing a crisp, sharp-angled check mark.

### Showcase Panels (Featured Showcase Module)
- Large top header bar with a stylized diagonal slash separator (`/// FEATURED ARTWORK` or `/// BADGE COLLECTION`).
- Polished manga screentone pattern applied as an ultra-subtle background watermark (`3%` opacity) across showcase backing plates.
